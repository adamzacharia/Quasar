# POL-05 HST exclusive access period, Cycle 34 (expect_web: true)

Question: "What is the default exclusive access period for HST GO data in Cycle 34?"

Key facts (C, 30), checked 2026-09-24 against hst-docs.stsci.edu, HST Call for Proposals for Cycle 34, "HST Data Rights and Duplications":
- Very Small, Small, Medium GO and SNAP programs: normally 6 months after the data are archived (15).
- Treasury and Large programs have no exclusive access period by default; the Hubble-Roman Science Initiative and HWO Precursor Science Initiative proposals also have none (10; either earns 6).
- Proposers may request a shorter 3-month period or waive exclusive access (5).

Official source for S / "official first": hst-docs.stsci.edu or stsci.edu.
