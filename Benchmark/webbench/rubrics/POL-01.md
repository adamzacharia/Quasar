# POL-01 ALMA Cycle 13 proprietary period (expect_web: true)

Question: "What is the proprietary period for ALMA Cycle 13 data?"

Key facts (C, 30), checked 2026-09-24 against the Cycle 13 Proposer's Guide and ALMA Users' Policies (Doc 13.16, March 2026) on almascience.org / .eso.org / .nrao.edu:
- 12 months (one year) for regular proposals (15).
- The clock starts when the data are delivered to the PI (per MOUS: after the raw data of the first EB of the MOUS are made available) (8).
- DDT exception: approved Cycle 13 DDT proposals have no proprietary period by default (up to 6 months on request) (7).
