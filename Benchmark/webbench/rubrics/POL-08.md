# POL-08 ALMA Cycle 13 Large Program threshold (expect_web: true)

Question: "What is the Large Program threshold in the ALMA Cycle 13 Call for Proposals?"

Key facts (C, 30), checked 2026-09-24 against the Cycle 13 Proposer's Guide (almascience.org/documents-and-tools/cycle13/alma-proposers-guide, PDF):
- Large Programs are proposals with an estimated execution time greater than 50 hours on the 12-m Array (with or without accompanying ACA time) or 150 hours on the 7-m Array in stand-alone mode (20; the 50 h figure alone earns 12).
- Regular proposals are those whose execution time does not exceed those limits (5).
- Context: Cycle 13 proposal deadline was 15:00 UTC on Thursday 23 April 2026 (Cycle 13 Proposer's Guide, Doc 13.2 ver. 1.0, 19 March 2026) (5).

Official source for S / "official first": almascience.org (or its mirrors). A Cycle 7 to Cycle 12 Proposer's Guide cited first counts as "not official first" for this Cycle 13 question.
