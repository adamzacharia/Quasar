# TRN-02 GRB 250702B (expect_web: true)

Question: "What is known so far about GRB 250702B?"

Key facts (C, 30), checked 2026-09-24 (ADS 2025ApJ...990L..28L, arXiv:2509.22792 / MNRAS 545, ESO eso2514):
- Detected by Fermi GBM on 2025 July 2; triggered repeatedly over several hours (250702B/D/E); day-long activity; longest-duration GRB known (12).
- Einstein Probe saw soft X-rays hours before the gamma-ray triggers; extragalactic, in a dusty host galaxy (8; either fact earns 4).
- Progenitor debate: incompatible with standard collapsar / merger GRBs; proposed models include a black hole falling into a stripped helium star and a tidal disruption by an intermediate-mass black hole (10).
