# NOS-01 Jeans mass derivation (expect_web: false)

Question: "Derive the Jeans mass for an isothermal, uniform-density gas cloud."

T (40): textbook physics, no web search.

Key facts (C, 50): virial or energy argument (or linear perturbation analysis) with sound speed c_s^2 = kT/(mu m_H) (15); Jeans length lambda_J ~ c_s sqrt(pi/(G rho)) (15); Jeans mass M_J ~ rho lambda_J^3, scaling as T^(3/2) rho^(-1/2), with a stated order-unity prefactor convention, e.g. (5kT/(G mu m_H))^(3/2) (3/(4 pi rho))^(1/2) (20).
