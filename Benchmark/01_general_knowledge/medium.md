## GK-M-01: Types of Downloadable ALMA Data

**Question:**
> What types of data can I download for observations made with ALMA?

**Skills Tested:**
- Understanding of ALMA data product hierarchy
- Knowledge of raw vs. calibrated vs. science-ready products
- RAG retrieval quality

**Expected Tools:**
- RAG pipeline (documentation retrieval)
- Possibly ALMA Science Archive browsing

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- [ ] Distinguishes between **raw data** (ASDM — ALMA Science Data Model) and **calibrated data**
- [ ] Mentions **pipeline-calibrated Measurement Sets (MS)**
- [ ] Mentions **science-ready data products** (images, cubes, continuum maps)
- [ ] References **auxiliary data** (pipeline weblog, scripts, calibration tables, README)
- [ ] Optionally mentions **quality assurance (QA2) reports**
- [ ] Mentions that availability of products depends on the project and cycle
- [ ] Factually accurate

## GK-M-02: ALMA Observing Bands

**Question:**
> How many observing bands are available with ALMA? How many bands have public data available in the ALMA Science Archive as of March 25, 2026?

**Skills Tested:**
- Factual knowledge of ALMA receiver bands
- Live archive query capability to check current data availability
- Distinguishing between "available for use" vs. "has public data"

**Expected Tools:**
- RAG pipeline (for band definitions)
- ALMA Science Archive query (TAP or web) to check which bands have public data

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- [ ] States the total number of ALMA receiver bands (10 bands: Band 1–10, though not all are commissioned)
- [ ] Correctly identifies which bands are currently commissioned and offered for observations
- [ ] Attempts a live query or provides recent knowledge about which bands have public data in the archive
- [ ] Distinguishes between "bands that exist" and "bands with public data in the archive"
- [ ] Provides band frequency ranges for at least the most common bands
- [ ] Factually accurate
