# ALMA Observing Proposal: Mapping the Dust Continuuum in HD 163296

## Scientific Justification
We aim to map the protoplanetary disk around the Herbig Ae star HD 163296. 
Our primary goal is to identify narrow gap structures in the dust continuum 
that could indicate the presence of giant planet formation.

## Technical Feasibility
We request Band 6 observations at 230 GHz.
We need an angular resolution of 0.05 arcsec to resolve the gaps.
To achieve this, we need an RMS sensitivity of 10 microJansky per beam.
We estimate this will require an integration time of only 2.5 hours on source.
