# Refund Policy
**Effective Date: May 24, 2026**
**Version: 2.0**

Quasar Observatory ("we," "us," or "our") operates the QUASAR Astronomical Assistant platform. This Refund Policy explains our billing, renewal, and cancellation terms.

---

## 1. Current Academic Free Tier Status

Quasar is currently provided to the public scientific and academic community as a free, open-source-backed astronomical research platform. 
* **No Billing Active:** We do not currently collect credit card information, charge access fees, or process financial transactions.
* **No Charges:** Since all active account tiers are free, no refund requests can be processed.

---

## 2. Future Paid Subscription Tiers

Should Quasar introduce paid subscription tiers or institutional licensing plans in the future, the following terms will apply immediately to all financial transactions:

### 2.1 Billing and Payments
* **Payment Gateways:** All financial transactions will be processed securely through certified PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment providers (e.g., Stripe). We will never store or directly access your credit card numbers.
* **Billing Cycle:** Subscriptions will be billed on a recurring basis (monthly or annually) according to your selected plan. Billing occurs at the start of your billing cycle.

### 2.2 Auto-Renewal & Cancellations
* **Auto-Renewal:** To ensure uninterrupted access to the Conductor DAG pipeline and personal document RAG storage, all paid subscriptions will renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle unless you cancel your subscription before your renewal date.
* **Cancellation Control:** You can cancel your paid subscription at any time through your Account Settings. Upon cancellation, your account will remain active at its current tier until the end of your prepaid billing period, at which point it will automatically downgrade. No further charges will be made.

### 2.3 Refund Qualification & Out-of-Scope Exceptions
* **General Non-Refundability:** Except as required by applicable local consumer protection laws (e.g., European Union 14-day cooling-off periods), all subscription fees, enterprise seats, and prepaid compute hours are non-refundable.
* **Service Interruptions:** Temporary outages, archive API downtime (e.g., ALMA/MAST servers going offline), or unverified AI hallucinations are out-of-scope for refund qualifications.
* **Chargebacks:** Any fraudulent chargebacks initiated without contacting our support team first will result in the immediate and permanent suspension of your authenticated Quasar account.

---

## 3. European Union 14-Day Cooling-off Period

If you are a resident of the European Union or European Economic Area, you have a statutory right to cancel a digital subscription within 14 days of your initial purchase without giving any reason. If you exercise this right, we will issue a full refund of your payment.
